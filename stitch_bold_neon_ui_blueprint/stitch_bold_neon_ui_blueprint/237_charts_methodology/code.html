Methodology

Version 1.0 · Published rules, no human picks.

How Songs Are Ranked

Every Monday at 10:00 WAT, code scores each eligible song by its share of weekly activity across YouTube (60%) and Audiomack (40%).

Tracking runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 WAT. Numbers reflect worldwide listening to Cameroonian music.

Eligibility
  • Verified Cameroonian primary artist
    Born in Cameroon, holds nationality, or based in Cameroon.
  • Official channels only
    Fan re-uploads, lyric copies, and sped-up edits do not count.
  • 104-week release window
    Songs leave after 52 weeks on chart if they fall below #25.
How Fake Plays Are Handled

Sudden 10× view spikes without matching likes or comments are automatically capped at 3× the song's 7-day average.

Any capped song is marked "adjusted for irregular activity" on the chart.
